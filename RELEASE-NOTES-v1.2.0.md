# Version 1.2.0 — preprint

- **Release date:** 2026-09-02
- **Publication status:** preprint

## Mathematical sources

The two Liebeck--Praeger--Saxl results used in the proof have been checked
against the primary papers. Corollary 3(iv) of the 2000 paper applies to the
constructed primitive product action and yields the required core-free
transitive subgroup in its almost-simple component. Theorem D and Remark 2
of the 1990 memoir apply to the factorization involving the 6-set stabilizer
for \(A_n\), with \(n\geq13\), and give the contradiction used in the
alternating-group argument. The main theorem is stated unconditionally.

## Manuscript

The manuscript has been reorganized around the reduction to a monolithic
quotient and the resulting primitive product action. The abstract and
introduction now state the classification, its central mechanism, and the
external classification inputs directly. Representative obstruction
arguments remain in the main body, while the family-by-family applications
and finite computations appear in conventional appendices.

## Computation

GAP 4.15.1 and TomLib 1.2.11 verify the fifteen named finite coordinate
cases used in the proof. The repository also contains nine independent
checks of sporadic cases and the AtlasRep calculation for the \(q=4\)
symplectic example. The release assets contain the 16-page manuscript,
source archive, and SHA-256 sidecar.
