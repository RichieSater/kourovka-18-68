# Version 1.1.0 — preprint

- **Release date:** 2026-08-31
- **Publication status:** preprint

## Mathematical status

The main theorem is proved using CFSG and the published classification and
factorization results cited in the manuscript. Corollary 3(iv) of
Liebeck--Praeger--Saxl (2000) applies to the constructed primitive product
action, and Theorem D with Remark 2 of Liebeck--Praeger--Saxl (1990) applies
to the 6-subset stabilizer used for alternating groups of degree at least 13.

## Changes in this version

- The sporadic-family argument uses Giudici's published results as follows:
  Theorem 1.1 and Tables 1--2 cover simple sporadic groups, Theorem 1.2 and
  Table 3 cover the new outer cases, and Theorem 1.3 and Table 4 list exact
  factorizations. Factor-freeness uses the complete lists in Tables 1--3.
- The Levchuk--Likharev occurrence citation identifies Theorem 1 and Lemmas 4
  and 6.
- The monolithic reduction includes the kernel argument proving that the
  unique minimal normal subgroup is self-centralizing.
- The official July 2026 Kourovka Notebook PDF and a functioning Cambridge
  Chapter 8 locator replace stale links.
- The deterministic build documentation and executable metadata use epoch
  `1788134400`.

## Reproducible computation

The repository contains data for 414 TomLib tables, 2,395 maximal-subgroup
classes, 73 `CMP`-positive tables, and 24 factor-free rows. The release assets
contain the 20-page PDF, source archive, and SHA-256 sidecar.
