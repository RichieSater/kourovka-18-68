# Version 1.0.2 — proof correction

- **Release date:** 2026-08-11
- **Artifact status:** historical; not a current deliverable

## Supersession notice

Versions 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 are superseded and do not contain the complete proof.

Their graph-outer \(\operatorname{PSp}_4(2^f)\) construction asserted that an
orthogonal-subgroup normalizer was maximal and supplemented the socle for
every almost-simple coordinate group. The assertion is false at

\[
X=\operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{PSp}_4(4))=S_4(4).4.
\]

GAP/AtlasRep gives index \(272\) for the former subgroup and index \(2\) for
the group generated by that subgroup and the socle. Thus it is neither
maximal nor a socle supplement. This was a material proof gap.

## Replacement proof

For \(S=\operatorname{Sp}_4(2^f)\), version 1.0.2 chooses a prime
\(r\mid f\), sets \(d=f/r\), and uses the standard subfield subgroup

\[
V=\operatorname{Sp}_4(2^d)<S.
\]

The corrected argument:

1. cites prime-extension subfield maximality;
2. proves that the exceptional graph-field generator normalizes \(V\);
3. proves that \(H=N_X(V)\) is maximal, core-free, satisfies \(X=SH\), and
   has \(H\cap S=V\) for every
   \(S\leq X\leq\operatorname{Aut}(S)\);
4. proves that all three involution classes meet
   \(\operatorname{Sp}_4(2)\leq V\); and
5. applies
   \[
   v_2(\lvert X:H\rvert)=4(f-d)>v_2(\lvert X:S\rvert)
   \]
   to exclude regular subgroups for every direct-power multiplicity.

At \(q=4\) and \(X=\operatorname{Aut}(S)\), the corrected regression data
are

\[
\lvert X:H\rvert=1360,\qquad
\lvert H\rvert=2880,\qquad
\lvert H\cap S\rvert=720.
\]

All three involution classes meet \(H\cap S\).

## Other mathematical and reproducibility changes

- The \(k=1\) coordinate obstruction proves core-freeness using almost
  simplicity instead of asserting it for arbitrary regular subgroups.
- The classification details identify every classical maximal-factorization
  row, graph automorphism, triality case, low parameter, and published
  correction.
- The GAP and Python programs reproduce the finite calculations.
- The deterministic PDF build records its toolchain and input hashes.

The proof uses CFSG and the named exhaustive classification results. BHRD
Table 8.14 supplies the subfield maximality statement, also recorded by the
cited Burness and Harper sources.
