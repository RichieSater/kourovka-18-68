## Supersession notice

Versions 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 are preserved for provenance but are superseded and
must not be cited as containing the complete proof.

Their graph-outer $\operatorname{PSp}_4(2^f)$ construction asserted that an
orthogonal-subgroup normalizer was maximal and supplemented the socle for
every almost-simple coordinate group. The assertion is false at

\[
X=\operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{PSp}_4(4))=S_4(4).4.
\]

GAP/AtlasRep gives index 272 for the old subgroup and index 2 for the group
generated by that subgroup and the socle. Thus it is neither maximal nor a
socle supplement. This was an acceptance-level gap, not a cosmetic change.

## Replacement proof

For $S=\operatorname{Sp}_4(2^f)$, version 1.0.2 chooses a prime $r\mid f$,
sets $d=f/r$, and uses the standard subfield subgroup

\[
V=\operatorname{Sp}_4(2^d)<S.
\]

The corrected manuscript:

1. cites prime-extension subfield maximality;
2. proves that the exceptional graph-field generator normalizes $V$;
3. proves that $H=N_X(V)$ is maximal, core-free, and satisfies
   $X=SH$ and $H\cap S=V$ for every
   $S\leq X\leq\operatorname{Aut}(S)$;
4. proves that all three involution classes meet
   $\operatorname{Sp}_4(2)\leq V$; and
5. applies
   \[
   v_2(|X:H|)=4(f-d)>v_2(|X:S|)
   \]
   to exclude regular subgroups for every direct-power multiplicity.

At $q=4$ and $X=\operatorname{Aut}(S)$, the new regression data are

\[
|X:H|=1360,\qquad |H|=2880,\qquad |H\cap S|=720.
\]

All three involution classes meet $H\cap S$.
